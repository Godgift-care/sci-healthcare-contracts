# Ƙamus na kalmomin fasaha

Gajeren ƙamus da jagororin Hausa ke bi. Wasu kalmomi suna nan da Turanci saboda haka suke bayyana a cikin manhajar, walat da kwangilar; an fassara sauran haka a kowane wuri. Cikakken bayani yana cikin [ƙamus na Turanci](../glossary.md).

| Turanci | Hausa | Bayani |
| --- | --- | --- |
| voucher | takardar jinya | Kuɗin da aka biya don takamaiman aiki a takamaiman asibiti. |
| funder | mai biya | Wanda ya biya kuɗin takardar. |
| clinic / provider | asibiti / mai ba da sabis | Asibiti, kantin magani ko lab da ke karɓar biya. |
| patient | majiyyaci | Wanda ke karɓar jinya. |
| attester | mai tabbatarwa | Wanda aka ba izini ya tabbatar an ba da jinya. |
| attest | tabbatarwa | Kiran `attest` da ke buɗe lokacin ƙorafi. |
| administrator (admin) | mai gudanarwa | Wanda ke tantance asibitoci da naɗa masu tabbatarwa. |
| escrow | ajiyar amana | Kuɗin da kwangilar ke riƙe wa har sai an biya ko an mayar. |
| dispute | ƙorafi | Ƙin yarda da ke daskare kuɗin har mai gudanarwa ya yanke hukunci. |
| dispute window | lokacin ƙorafi | Awanni 72 bayan tabbatarwa. |
| settlement / settle | biya (ga asibiti) | Tura kuɗin zuwa asibiti bayan lokacin ƙorafi. |
| refund | mayar da kuɗi | Komawar kuɗin zuwa wajen mai biya. |
| expiry | ranar ƙarewa | Ranar da ba za a iya karɓar takardar ba kuma. |
| protocol fee | kuɗin sabis na tsarin | 1% na adadin takardar. |
| wallet | walat | Manhajar da ke riƙe makullin asusunku na Stellar. |
| trustline | trustline | Ba a fassara ba: sunan da Stellar da walat ke amfani da shi. |
| smart contract | kwangila | Shirin da ke gudana a kan blockchain. |
| blockchain | blockchain | Ba a fassara ba. |
| secret key | kalmar sirri | Sirrin da mai biya da majiyyaci suka raba. |
| receipt | rasit | Shaidar da ake ƙirƙira lokacin biyan asibiti. |

Sunayen matsayi (`Funded`, `Claimed`, `Attested`, `Settled`, `Refunded`, `Pending`, `Active`) da maɓallan manhajar (**My vouchers**, **Refund me**, **Dispute** da sauransu) suna nan da Turanci domin su yi daidai da abin da manhajar ke nunawa.
