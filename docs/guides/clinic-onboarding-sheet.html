Getting Started — 4 Steps

1. Connect & Register
Open the Clinic Desk in your browser. Connect your Stellar wallet (Freighter, Albedo, Lobstr). Enter clinic name and 2-letter country code. You are now Pending.
2. Get Verified
An administrator reviews your licence, location, and contact. Once approved, you move to Active. Only Active clinics can list services and receive vouchers.
3. List Services
Add each service: a code (number), label (e.g. "Outpatient consult"), and price in USDC. Keep labels coarse — no diagnoses on chain. Prices are a floor; funders may pay more.
4. Get Paid
Patient arrives → Mark patient seen (claim) → Deliver care → Attester confirms → 72-hour dispute window → Release payment (settle). You receive voucher amount minus 1% protocol fee.

Key Rules to Remember

  • You cannot attest your own vouchers — the contract rejects it even if you hold an attester role.
  • Claim promptly — a voucher cannot be claimed after it expires (funder gets it back).
  • Settlement is permissionless — once the dispute window closes, anyone can trigger it. No operator can withhold your payment.
  • If suspended: you keep vouchers already funded. You cannot receive new ones or change prices.
  • Payment is in USDC. Convert to local currency via a Stellar anchor (Yellowcard, Cowrie, HoneyCoin, MoneyGram) — separate KYC step.
  • Stellar network fees (fractions of a cent in XLM) are paid by whoever submits the transaction.

Disputes & Refunds

Funder disputes (within 72 hrs of attestation):
Funds freeze in escrow. Admin resolves → funder or clinic.
Admin cannot send money to a third party.
No attestation?
Unclaimed: refund at expiry (or immediately if clinic inactive).
Claimed but never attested: refund 7 days after expiry.
No fee on refunds.
