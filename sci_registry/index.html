§SCI Healthcare — Provider Registry

Answers one question for the rest of the protocol: who is allowed to do what. It holds verified care providers, the coarse service categories each one is approved to bill for, and the set of attesters permitted to confirm that care was delivered.

No patient-identifying data is stored here, or anywhere else in this protocol. Services are coarse categories, never diagnoses.
