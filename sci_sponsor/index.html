§SCI Healthcare — Sponsorship desk

Lets a sponsor put a budget behind someone else’s voucher. The sponsor deposits a budget, sets the rules it wants, and the desk adds a second voucher for the same care — funded by the sponsor — whenever a voucher qualifies.

§Why a second voucher and not a bigger one

The match is an ordinary voucher, created through the voucher contract and escrowed there. Nothing about the care lifecycle is re-implemented here: the clinic claims it, an attester confirms it, the funder can dispute it, and the money is settled or refunded by the same code that already handles every other voucher. This contract only decides whether a match happens and how much it costs the sponsor.

§Why the money sits here

The matching voucher is funded by this contract, so the sponsor’s budget is escrowed by the voucher contract exactly like a funder’s own money. The sponsor’s budget is the difference between what it deposited and what it has committed to matches; whatever is left can be withdrawn.

§What this contract is not

It is not a source of care. A match never creates a claimable right on its own: the money is only ever spent when the clinic delivers and an attester confirms it, exactly as for any other voucher. The abuse cases this design has to answer are written up in the sponsor section of docs/developers/contracts.md.
