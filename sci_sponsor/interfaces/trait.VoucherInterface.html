fn voucher_summary( env: Env, voucher_id: u64, ) -> Option<(BytesN<32>, Address, Address, u32, i128, u32, u64, u64)>

The fields of a voucher this contract needs to match it, in one call.

(beneficiary_ref, funder, provider, service_code, amount, status, created_at, expires_at), where status is the voucher’s VoucherStatus as a number: 0 Funded, 1 Claimed, 2 Attested, 3 Settled, 4 Disputed, 5 Refunded. None when the voucher does not exist.
